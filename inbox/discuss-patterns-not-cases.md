# Discuss patterns, not cases

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Real commercial context can be useful when discussing technology, but identifiable cases should not become the teaching material.

Share architectures, workflows, failure modes, review strategies, and generalized constraints. Remove client identity, proprietary details, unique timelines, internal metrics, private repositories, and other facts that could reconstruct the underlying case.

# 中文

## 目前表述

技術討論可以帶有真實商業場景，但不要把可識別的個案本身拿來當教材。

可以公開架構、流程、失敗模式、Review 方法與抽象後的限制條件；但應移除客戶身分、專有細節、獨特時間線、內部數據、私有 Repo，以及任何足以讓第三人還原原始案例的資訊。
