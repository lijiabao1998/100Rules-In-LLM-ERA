# Persistent context turns work into rendering

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Once the relevant context, constraints, state, and output schema are persistent, many tasks stop being acts of reconstruction and become acts of rendering.

The expensive part of repeated knowledge work is often not producing the artifact; it is rebuilding the state required to produce it.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

當相關上下文、限制條件、狀態與輸出格式都能持久保存時，很多工作就不再是「重新建模」，而只是「把既有狀態渲染成輸出」。

重複知識工作真正昂貴的部分，往往不是產出文件本身，而是每次都要重新建立能產出它的上下文。
