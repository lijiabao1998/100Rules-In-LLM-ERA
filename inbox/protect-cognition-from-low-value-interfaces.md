# Protect scarce cognition from low-value interfaces

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A low-value interface becomes expensive when it repeatedly forces high-value cognition to context-switch, rehearse, and reprocess the same uncertainty.

Reduce the interface, externalize the state, and reserve human attention for genuine deltas and irreversible decisions.
