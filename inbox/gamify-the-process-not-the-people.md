# Gamify the process, not the people

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Gamification can make repetitive or high-friction work easier to sustain, but the scoring target matters.

Treat completed loops, clean records, clarified rules, reduced uncertainty, and closed exceptions as the "wins." Do not turn real people into enemies that must be defeated.

This keeps motivation high without letting conflict distort the model.

# 中文

## 目前表述

遊戲化可以降低重複或高摩擦流程的心理成本，但關鍵在於「計分對象」。

把完成閉環、乾淨留痕、規則澄清、不確定性下降、例外結案視為勝利；不要把現實中的人本身設計成必須被擊敗的敵人。

這樣既能保留動力，也不會讓衝突反過來污染模型。
