# Do not enter a high-friction interface without observability

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When a synchronous interaction carries meaningful risk, do not rely on memory, improvisation, or ambiguous verbal exchange.

Enter only when the objective, boundaries, and recording method are clear. Preserve a contemporaneous record of what was said, what changed, what was agreed, and what remains unresolved.

# 中文

## 目前表述

當同步互動具有明顯摩擦或風險時，不要依賴記憶、臨場 improvisation 或模糊口頭交換。

只有在目標、邊界與留痕方式都清楚時才進場；同步保存當下紀錄，固定誰說了什麼、什麼被改變、什麼已確認，以及什麼仍未解決。
