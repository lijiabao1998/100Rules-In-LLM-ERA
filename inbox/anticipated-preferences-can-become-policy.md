# Anticipated preferences can become policy without an order

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Organizations do not need an explicit command for a superior's perceived preference to shape behavior.

When lower levels infer what will be rewarded, tolerated, or punished, they may over-implement an imagined preference. The resulting behavior can exceed what the principal actually wanted and create costs for both sides.

# 中文

## 目前表述

一個組織不需要出現明確命令，上級「被感知到的偏好」就可能影響行為。

當下層開始揣測什麼會被獎勵、容忍或追責時，可能把想像中的偏好執行過頭；最後形成的行為甚至可能超過上級真正想要的程度，並同時給上下兩端製造成本。
