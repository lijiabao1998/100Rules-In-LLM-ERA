# Study the mechanism without consuming the suffering

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When a real-world problem contains human suffering, model the policy mechanism rather than turning the suffering itself into the product.

Abstract enough to study trade-offs, incentives, and system behavior; do not rely on real victims, sensational framing, or gamified pain to make the work compelling.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

當真實世界的問題包含人的苦難時，應研究政策機制，而不是把苦難本身做成產品。

抽象到足以研究取捨、誘因與系統行為即可；不要依賴真實受害者、獵奇敘事或遊戲化的痛苦，來讓作品變得有吸引力。
