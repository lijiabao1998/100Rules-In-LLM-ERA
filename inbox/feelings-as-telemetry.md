# Treat feelings as telemetry, not the objective function

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A feeling can be real and important without becoming the system's objective function.

Use emotion as telemetry about cost, friction, and boundary violations. Let the final action optimize for the larger goal.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

一種感受可以是真實而重要的，但不必因此成為整個系統的目標函數。

把情緒當成成本、摩擦與邊界受損的遙測訊號；真正的行動，仍然服務更大的最終目標。
