# Build for a horizon longer than the market cycle

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A project can be worth continuing even before it proves commercial demand if the act of building it compounds capability, taste, history, and optionality over a long horizon.

Not every durable project needs to justify itself on the timescale of a product launch.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

即使一個專案尚未證明商業需求，只要持續建造本身能長期累積能力、品味、歷史與選擇權，它仍然值得繼續。

不是每一個耐久專案，都必須用一次產品上市的時間尺度來證明自己。
