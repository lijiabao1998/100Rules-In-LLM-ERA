# Use empathy to simulate constraints, not to certify motives

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Empathy can improve prediction when it is used to reconstruct another person's constraints, incentives, uncertainty, and available actions.

But a plausible simulation is still a model, not evidence of the person's actual motive.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

共情可以提升判斷力，前提是它被用來重建另一個人的限制、誘因、不確定性與可選行動。

但再合理的情境模擬，也仍然只是模型，不能直接當成對方真實動機的證據。
