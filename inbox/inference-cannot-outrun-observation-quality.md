# Inference cannot outrun observation quality

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A strong reasoning system can maintain long context, compare hypotheses, and update consistently, but it cannot manufacture missing ground truth.

When the world is only partially observable, the quality of the conclusion is bounded by the quality, coverage, and calibration of the incoming observations.

# 中文

## 目前表述

再強的推理系統，也只能在已有觀測上維持長上下文、比較假設並一致更新；它不能憑空製造缺失的真實世界資料。

當真實世界只能被部分觀測時，結論品質的上限，取決於輸入資料的品質、覆蓋範圍與校準程度。
