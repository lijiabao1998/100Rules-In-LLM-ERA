# Disclose only what the interface requires

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Do not volunteer internal implementation detail merely because it exists.

Provide the minimum information necessary for the other side to make the decision, verify the result, or fulfill a legitimate obligation. Expand disclosure when the task, contract, law, or direct question genuinely requires it.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

不要因為內部資訊存在，就主動把它全部攤開。

只提供對方完成決策、驗證結果或履行正當義務所必要的資訊；只有在任務、契約、法律或直接問題確實需要時，才擴大披露範圍。
