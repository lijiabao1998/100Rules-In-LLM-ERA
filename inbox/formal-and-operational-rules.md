# Model formal rules and operational rules separately

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A system can have a formal rule and an operational rule at the same time.

Do not force policy text, observed behavior, incentives, exceptions, and local practice into one canonical description. Track them as separate data channels, then study where they converge, diverge, and change over time.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

一個系統可以同時存在正式規則與實際運作規則。

不要把政策文字、觀察到的行為、誘因、例外與地方慣例硬壓成單一真相。把它們當成不同資料通道分開記錄，再觀察它們何時一致、何時分岔，以及如何隨時間改變。
