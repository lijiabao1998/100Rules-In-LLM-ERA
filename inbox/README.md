# Inbox

The Inbox is a temporary home for ideas that may become rules.

Items here are not official rules.

They have no rule number and do not need to be complete.

An Inbox item may begin as:

- a single sentence
- an observation
- a pattern noticed in real life
- a principle that still needs testing
- two competing interpretations
- an idea that may later be merged into another rule

The purpose of the Inbox is capture, not classification.

Do not force an idea into a category too early.

When an item becomes strong enough, it may be:

- promoted into `/rules/`
- merged with another item
- rewritten
- kept for later
- dropped

Promotion is not based on age.

It is based on whether the idea survives discussion, evidence, counterexamples, and reality.

# 暫存區

Inbox 是那些「可能成為規則，但現在還不必決定」的想法暫存區。

放在這裡的內容不是正式規則。

它們沒有規則編號，也不需要完整。

一個 Inbox 項目可以只是：

- 一句話
- 一個觀察
- 現實中反覆出現的模式
- 一條仍然需要測試的原則
- 兩種互相競爭的解釋
- 未來可能與其他規則合併的想法

Inbox 的目的首先是捕獲，而不是分類。

不要太早讓一個想法進入某個分類。

當一個項目足夠成熟時，它可以：

- 升格到 `/rules/`
- 與其他項目合併
- 重寫
- 繼續保留觀察
- 放棄

是否升格，不取決於它放了多久。

而取決於它能不能經得起討論、證據、反例與現實。
