# Inbox

The Inbox is a temporary home for ideas that may become rules.

Items here are not official rules.

They have no rule number and do not need to be complete.

An Inbox item may begin as:

- a single sentence
- an observation
- a pattern noticed in real life
- a principle that still needs testing
- two competing interpretations
- an idea that may later be merged into another rule

The purpose of the Inbox is capture, not classification.

Do not force an idea into a category too early.

When an item becomes strong enough, it may be:

- promoted into `/rules/`
- merged with another item
- rewritten
- kept for later
- dropped

Promotion is not based on age.

It is based on whether the idea survives discussion, evidence, counterexamples, and reality.

## Auto-capture boundary

Inbox may be updated automatically when a reusable, non-sensitive insight emerges.

Automatic capture is limited to `/inbox/`.

Do not automatically publish or preserve:

- client, SEO, pricing, or business-operation details
- private project names, internal company information, or unpublished work
- personal health details
- immigration, legal, or administrative case specifics
- private conversations, contact details, or identifying information
- passwords, credentials, permissions, or secrets
- any detail that could allow a third party to reconstruct a private event

Insights derived from sensitive contexts may be captured only after they have been abstracted enough that the original private event cannot reasonably be reconstructed.

Automatic capture does not authorize promotion into `/rules/`.

Promotion remains a human decision.

# 暫存區

Inbox 是那些「可能成為規則，但現在還不必決定」的想法暫存區。

放在這裡的內容不是正式規則。

它們沒有規則編號，也不需要完整。

一個 Inbox 項目可以只是：

- 一句話
- 一個觀察
- 現實中反覆出現的模式
- 一條仍然需要測試的原則
- 兩種互相競爭的解釋
- 未來可能與其他規則合併的想法

Inbox 的目的首先是捕獲，而不是分類。

不要太早讓一個想法進入某個分類。

當一個項目足夠成熟時，它可以：

- 升格到 `/rules/`
- 與其他項目合併
- 重寫
- 繼續保留觀察
- 放棄

是否升格，不取決於它放了多久。

而取決於它能不能經得起討論、證據、反例與現實。

## 自動捕獲邊界

當對話中出現可重複利用、且不涉及敏感資訊的洞見時，可以自動寫入 Inbox。

自動寫入權限僅限 `/inbox/`。

下列內容不得自動公開或保存：

- 客戶、SEO、報價或業務操作細節
- 私人專案名稱、公司內部資料或尚未公開的工作內容
- 個人健康細節
- 移民、法律或行政案件的具體內容
- 私人對話、聯絡方式或身分識別資訊
- 密碼、權限、憑證或其他秘密
- 任何足以讓第三人反推出私人事件的細節

若洞見來自敏感情境，只有在充分抽象化、使第三人無法合理重建原始私人事件時，才可以進入 Inbox。

自動捕獲不代表可以自行升格到 `/rules/`。

是否升格，仍由人類決定。
