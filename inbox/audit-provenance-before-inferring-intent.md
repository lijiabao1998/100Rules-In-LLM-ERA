# Audit provenance before inferring intent

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When a hostile-looking decision appears at a higher level, inspect the provenance of the information before inferring hostile intent.

Bad lower-level inputs can propagate upward and produce harmful decisions without the higher level ever holding the same motive or even understanding the original person accurately.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

當較高層級出現看似帶有敵意的決策時，先追查資訊來源，再推論其主觀意圖。

錯誤、偏差或惡性的底層材料可以向上傳遞，形成有害決策，而較高層未必具有相同動機，甚至未必真正理解原始當事人。
