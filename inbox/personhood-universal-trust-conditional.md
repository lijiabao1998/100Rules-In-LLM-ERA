# Keep personhood universal, make trust conditional

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Keep personhood universal. Make trust, cooperation, and discretion conditional on behavior.

Do not dehumanize someone in order to hold them accountable. Accountability becomes clearer when responsibility remains attached to a person capable of choice.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

人的基本人格應保持普遍；信任、合作與裁量則應依行為而定。

不需要先把一個人去人化，才能追究其責任。當責任仍然附著在一個能夠選擇、能夠理解後果的人身上，追責反而更清楚。
