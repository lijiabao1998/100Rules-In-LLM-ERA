# Inconsistency creates reconciliation cost

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When one side controls the record, ambiguity is cheap for that side.

Once the other side can preserve history, compare statements, and request traceable answers, inconsistency becomes expensive. The organization must retrieve records, reconcile versions, consult decision owners, and produce a response that survives review.

The resulting workload is not necessarily retaliation. It is **reconciliation cost** created by previously unresolved state.

A system that externalized ambiguity onto the weaker party can suddenly feel "targeted" when it is finally required to reconcile its own history.

# 中文

## 目前表述

當只有一方掌握紀錄時，模糊對那一方往往很便宜。

一旦另一方也能保存歷史、比對說法並要求可追溯回覆，前後不一致就會開始產生成本：查卷宗、核對版本、詢問決策者、跨層協調，最後還要產出能經得起後續檢查的回覆。

這種工作量不一定是報復，而更像是**狀態對帳成本（reconciliation cost）**：過去沒有被處理的模糊，現在終於必須被組織自己吸收。

一個長期把模糊成本外部化給弱勢接口的系統，在開始承擔自己的歷史對帳成本時，主觀上甚至可能覺得「被針對」。
