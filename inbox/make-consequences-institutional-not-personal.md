# Make consequences institutional, not personal

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When an interface behaves better only after it perceives resistance, the goal is not to create fear of personal retaliation. The safer and more durable mechanism is to make accountability salient:

- the record is accurate;
- contradictions are noticed;
- decisions are traceable;
- escalation routes are known;
- written evidence persists.

The strongest deterrence is often not "this person may hurt me," but "this person will notice, document, and route errors into a system that can review them."

# 中文

## 目前表述

如果一個接口只有在感受到阻力後才開始變得謹慎，目標不應是製造對私人報復的恐懼，而是讓可追責性變得顯著：

- 紀錄會被核對；
- 前後矛盾會被發現；
- 決定可以追溯；
- 升級路徑是已知的；
- 書面證據會持續存在。

更耐久的威懾通常不是「這個人可能傷害我」，而是「這個人會發現、記錄，並把錯誤送進可審查的制度流程」。
