# Suspend inference when state quality drops

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

A degraded state is data about your processing capacity, not necessarily data about the world.

Accept the state. Postpone further inference until your baseline returns.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

狀態下降首先是關於自身處理能力的資料，不必立刻被解讀成外部世界的證據。

接受狀態下降；暫停繼續向下推理，等基線恢復後再判斷。
