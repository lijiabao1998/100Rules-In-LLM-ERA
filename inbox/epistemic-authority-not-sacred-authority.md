# Do not let epistemic authority become sacred authority

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Reverence for intelligence can become dangerous when epistemic authority turns into sacred authority.

Respect the quality of an answer without making the source infallible.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

當對智慧的敬重，把「知識上的權威」轉化成「神聖不可質疑的權威」時，就可能發展成類宗教結構。

可以敬重答案的品質，但不要因此把答案的來源變成不可質疑。
