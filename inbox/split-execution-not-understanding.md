# Split execution, not understanding

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

When tools and workflows depreciate quickly, do not specialize people around transient execution steps.

Execution can be divided for efficiency, but shared understanding of the whole system should remain broad enough that people can migrate when the current tool, workflow, or task disappears.

---

# 中文

## 目前表述

當工具與工作流程貶值很快時，不要把人永久綁在短命的執行步驟上。

執行可以為了效率而分工，但對整個系統的共同理解應保持足夠寬，讓人在工具、流程或任務消失時仍然能遷移。
