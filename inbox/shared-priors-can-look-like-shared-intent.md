# Shared priors can look like shared intent

**Status:** Candidate

## Current wording

Repeatedly similar behavior across different people does not necessarily imply repeatedly independent hostile intent.

A shared case file, label, instruction, incentive, or organizational routine can create the appearance of coordinated intent even when individual motives differ.
